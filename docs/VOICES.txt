Voice: en_GB-southern_english_female-low
========================================

This voice is a Piper text-to-speech model ("southern_english_female",
low quality, 16 kHz), from the Piper project's release v0.0.2
(https://github.com/rhasspy/piper), converted from ONNX to pvoice's .voice
format (weights stored at half precision) by tools/onnx2voice.py.

It was trained on the "Crowdsourced high-quality UK and Ireland English
Dialect speech data set" (OpenSLR SLR83, https://www.openslr.org/83/), by
Isin Demirsahin, Oddur Kjartansson, Alexander Gutkin and Clara Rivera
(Google), and was fine-tuned from Piper's U.S. English "Ryan" voice.
